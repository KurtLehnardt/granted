## 1. Method

Two data sources, no new live spend (see the budget blocker above):

1. **Measured (free):** the 5 precomputed maps in `scaffold/data/precomputed.json` — the frozen,
   hand-verified demo-day results for the five judged test cases (`scaffold/lib/testCases.ts`), which
   correspond in substance to golden-set entries `01`–`05`. These were produced by the real pipeline
   (`buildOpportunityMap`) with `discernment_layer` **OFF** (the shipped default), so each match carries
   its raw `score`, `tier`, and `criteria`.
   - `scaffold/scripts/eval-precomputed.mjs` (added here, **runs free** — no API calls) reads those maps
     and reports, per case: the shipped honest-no (weak-field fires when `#{score≥33} < 1`) **and** the
     `discernment_layer`-ON view, by applying the **real** `recommendFor()` + `mapVerdict()` from
     `scaffold/lib/recommend.ts` to the same frozen scores. Because discernment is a *pure*
     post-processing pass over already-computed scores, this reproduces exactly what the layer would
     emit — no model re-run, no cost.

2. **Static analysis:** the calibration knobs (`scaffold/lib/match.ts` `CALIBRATION`), the discernment
   thresholds (`scaffold/lib/recommend.ts`), and the scoring + weak-field prompts
   (`scaffold/lib/prompts/registry.ts`).

The intended live component — an 8-profile adversarial set spanning strong-fit / borderline /
consumer-app / local-service / dating-app / oversized-ineligible / vague-one-liner / foreign-owned —

## 3. The discernment-layer finding (the substantive golden-set result)

**The layer does its intended job on the true negative and over-does it on two true positives.**

- **Correct on the no-fit case.** marketplace → `no_fit`, 0 recommend, 0 verify, `do_not_recommend` on
  all 35 candidates, max score 22. This is exactly the `do_not_recommend` / `no_fit` behavior the thesis
  wants, and it matches golden entry `education-05` ("matches nothing").
- **Over-conservative on legitimate cases 1 and 3.** health-IT (case 1) and water (case 3) each collapse
  to `thin_map` with **zero** recommended matches — which flips `summary.highPotential` to 0 and triggers
  the weak-field "you may be better served elsewhere" note (`match.ts`: `wantWeakField = verdict ∈
  {no_fit, thin_map}`). health-IT case 1 is the **flagship demo** and golden entry `health-it-01`, whose
  `taca_notes` say a good map "surfaces 5–8 concrete eligible SBIR/STTR paths." Rendering it as a thin
  "no strong matches" map is a clear quality regression.

**Root cause.** `RECOMMEND_FLOOR` (`recommend.ts`) requires **52** for a grant/rd match to be
*recommended*. But the anchored scoring rubric (`prompts/registry.ts`) defines **35–54 as "Partial /
verify — a real but incomplete or uncertain fit"** and **55–74 as "Strong — a genuine applicant fit."**
In practice the scorer places even good grant fits for health-IT and water in the *partial* band (both
top out at 42), so **no grant/rd match clears 52** and nothing gets recommended. Manufacturing and cyber
clear only because their best matches genuinely reach the 55–72 strong band (procurement/rd). So the
layer's aggressiveness is mis-tuned against the scorer's actual output distribution for grant-heavy
verticals.

**Recommendation (golden-set validation verdict on `discernment_layer`): DO NOT ENABLE as-is.**
Before flipping the flag: lower the grant/rd `RECOMMEND_FLOOR` toward the top of the observed
partial band (~40–42) — or, equivalently, re-tune the scorer to reach the strong band (55+) for
genuine SBIR/STTR fits — and re-validate the full 34-entry golden set so cases 1 and 3 recover a
non-empty recommended set while marketplace stays `no_fit`. This is report-only (the flag is already
OFF, so there is no live harm); I did not change `RECOMMEND_FLOOR` because that is itself a calibration
decision that needs a full-set live re-validation I could not run this session.

## 4. Why the honest-no is structurally robust (static evidence)

The behavior above is not luck — it is enforced at three layers, which is why I'm confident the
un-runnable adversarial cases (dating-app, consumer-app, local-service, foreign-owned, oversized, vague)
would also produce honest-nos:

1. **The scoring prompt is explicitly anti-fabrication.** The anchored `explainMatches` and
   `scoreMatches` prompts both contain, near-verbatim: *"BE WILLING TO SAY NO … Fabricating
   plausible-sounding matches is the single worst failure mode here. Consumer marketplaces, local
   service businesses, and companies with no R&D component frequently have no strong federal grant match
   — say so plainly / score them low and honestly."* They also band-anchor scores (0–20 no fit, 21–34
   adjacent, 35–54 partial, 55–74 strong) and require `whyIneligible` to carry *"SPECIFIC, REAL concerns
   … never boilerplate, never empty."*
2. **The strong floor sits above the adjacent/noise band.** `scoreFloor = 33` with
   `weakFieldThreshold = 1`: a case with zero matches at 33+ *is* an honest-no. The floor was
   deliberately raised 30→33 to keep case-5's borderline STEM grants out of the strong set.
3. **The weak-field finding is a first-class, well-formed output**, not an empty state — the
   `explainWeakField` prompt: *"deliver it with confidence, not apology … Do not hedge into implying
   they should apply anyway. The value here is saving them weeks of wasted applications,"* with 3–5
   concrete real redirects (SBA, state economic development, procurement, university partnerships).

**Projected behavior on the un-runnable adversarial set** (mechanism-based, *not* live-measured):
- **consumer-app / dating-app / local-service** (golden `consumer-17`, `education-05`): the marketplace
  case is a measured proxy — no R&D + no federal funding mechanism ⇒ low scores ⇒ weak-field / `no_fit`.
- **foreign-owned, non-R&D** (golden `defense-hw-08`): the `US-based small business` criterion goes
  unmet and the fit scores low; note the eligibility engine renders ownership as `unknown` (model-inferred),
  not a hard `excluded` (R8.4) — so the honest-no here comes from the **score**, and `whyIneligible`
  should name the ownership/US-presence concern.
- **oversized firm** (golden `dualuse-sw-12`): same — size is model-inferred, so it flows as `unknown`,
  and the honest-no depends on the scorer down-weighting an 8,000-employee multinational against a
  small-business mechanism. **This is the projection I'd most want to confirm live**, because a large
  firm with a real R&D story is the case most at risk of an over-generous rd/SBIR recommend. It is the
  top priority for the first credit-restored run.

Corroborating existing evidence: `evals/false-exclusion-eval.mjs` previously reported **zero false
exclusions** and zero R8.4/unknown-gate failures — i.e. the system does not fabricate *ineligibility*
either, the mirror-image of not fabricating fits.

## 6. Verdict on the thesis

**Does the honest-no thesis hold? YES — as shipped.** On the measured, hand-verified evidence, the
system says "there probably isn't a strong match" exactly when it should (the youth-marketplace case)
and does not fabricate or over-sell on the four cases that do fit. The behavior is backed by explicit
anti-fabrication prompt instructions, a strong-floor margin, and a first-class weak-field output — so
it is structural, not incidental.

**Two caveats travel with that yes:** (1) the more-aggressive `discernment_layer` must **stay OFF**
until its recommend floor is recalibrated (§3), and (2) this session's evidence is the **5 precomputed
cases plus static analysis** — the 29 dedicated adversarial golden entries could not be run live because
the account is credit-exhausted. Re-run `scaffold/scripts/eval-run.mjs` (and the full
`evals/golden-set.jsonl`) once credits are restored to convert the §4 projections into measurements;
the oversized-firm case is the highest-value one to confirm first.
