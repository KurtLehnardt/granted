System Architecture · v2

Granted — from a plain-English pitch
to a calibrated, honest funding map

One request streams through a seven-stage pipeline that retrieves real federal opportunities, scores fit against a program officer's bar, screens eligibility without ever fabricating a ruling, and, new in v2, tells a founder plainly when not to apply. Every model output is grounded or gated; every risky feature ships behind a default-off flag.
